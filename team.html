Partner · Energy Law & Environmental Compliance

Fredric Craigin Montfort

Fredric Craigin Montfort is a partner whose practice focuses on energy law and environmental compliance. His work addresses legal and regulatory considerations that may affect energy activities, commercial decisions, and environmental obligations. He helps clients understand requirements relevant to a matter, identify compliance questions, and assess how legal duties relate to operational or contractual choices. This can include reviewing the regulatory context, clarifying responsibilities, and considering practical steps for addressing a compliance concern or a change in requirements. Fredric focuses on clear legal analysis, careful attention to applicable obligations, and guidance that helps clients understand risks and make informed decisions in a complex area.

Partner · Technology & Intellectual Property

Deborah J. Dwyer

Deborah J. Dwyer is a partner whose practice focuses on technology and intellectual property law. Her work addresses the legal issues that arise as technology and creative assets are developed, owned, licensed, commercialized, and used in business. This can include questions about software, digital products, inventions, brands, creative works, confidential information, and the contracts that govern their use. Deborah helps clients assess rights and obligations, understand ownership or licensing questions, and consider options when disagreements arise. She aims to make the connection between technical details, commercial goals, and legal rules clear, so clients can weigh the implications and choose an appropriate path forward.

Partner · Contract Law & Commercial Disputes

Eunice Alberta Jackson

Eunice Alberta Jackson is a partner focused on contract law and commercial disputes. Her practice centers on how business agreements are created, interpreted, performed, and enforced. When a commercial relationship becomes contested, Eunice helps clients examine the agreement, relevant correspondence, performance history, and the issues each side raises. She works to clarify contractual duties, potential remedies, business risks, and the options for resolving the disagreement. Depending on the matter, a path forward may involve direct negotiation, structured dispute resolution, or litigation. Eunice aims to organize complex facts, explain available choices, and keep attention on client objectives while the matter progresses.

Partner · Human Rights Law & Litigation

Paul Roy Traub

Paul Roy Traub is a partner whose practice focuses on human rights law and litigation. His work addresses legal questions involving fundamental rights, individual protections, and the responsibilities of public and private actors. He helps clients define the issues in dispute, connect relevant facts to the legal questions, and understand possible routes for addressing a matter. In litigation, preparation can involve developing legal arguments, organizing documents and other supporting material, and explaining procedural steps as a case moves forward. Paul emphasizes thoughtful analysis, clear communication, and principled advocacy. He works to help clients understand the strategy, choices, and practical considerations involved throughout the process.
