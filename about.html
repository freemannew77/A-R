Our story

A steady hand for

Alder & Rowe was built around a simple idea: excellent legal counsel should feel both rigorous and human. For more than two decades, our attorneys have helped individuals, families, founders, and established businesses make consequential decisions with confidence.

We bring different perspectives and deep experience to each matter. We begin by listening, explain the choices in front of you, and build a plan around your goals. You’ll know who is working on your matter and what comes next.

01

Listen first

Every matter begins with understanding the people, pressures, and priorities behind it. We ask careful questions before we recommend a path.

02

Make it clear

We translate legal complexity into practical options. You receive candid advice about tradeoffs, timing, and likely outcomes.

03

Stay beside you

We keep communication direct and consistent. As circumstances change, we revisit the plan and help you move forward deliberately.
