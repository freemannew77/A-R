Our commitment

Making this site
easier to use.

Juniper Legal Partners, LLP is committed to improving the accessibility and usability of this website. We aim to make our content available to people with a range of abilities, devices, and assistive technologies.

We use the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA as a design and development target. This is an ongoing effort; we do not claim that every page or feature has been independently audited or currently conforms in every respect.

01

Keyboard access

Use the Tab key to move through links and controls, Enter to activate them, and Escape to close the mobile navigation menu. A visible focus outline helps show which item is selected.

02

Clear structure

Pages use headings, landmarks, and descriptive link text to support navigation with screen readers and other assistive tools. Decorative elements are hidden from assistive technology where appropriate.

03

Flexible display

The layout adapts to different screen sizes, and the site honors a device setting for reduced motion. You can also zoom the page using your browser controls.

Need information another way?

If you have difficulty using a page, need an alternative format, or notice an accessibility barrier, please contact us. Tell us which page you were using and what you were trying to do; please do not include confidential legal information in your message.

We welcome feedback and will work to provide the information or assistance you need. Contact details on this sample website are placeholders and must be replaced before publication.
